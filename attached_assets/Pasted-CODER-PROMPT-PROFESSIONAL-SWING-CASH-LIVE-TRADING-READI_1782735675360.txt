CODER PROMPT — PROFESSIONAL SWING CASH LIVE-TRADING READINESS + FAST EXECUTION SYSTEM

We now want to make the Swing Trading Cash Segment professional, accurate, reliable, and ready for controlled live trading.

Important context:

The swing audit showed the strategy has promising paper performance and a solid signal/risk framework, but it is NOT ready for blind autonomous real-money trading yet.

Current verdict:

* Suitable for small supervised manual-execution micro-pilot only.
* Not ready for full-size live trading.
* Not ready for autonomous broker order placement until execution, reconciliation, risk, event, sector, and data-safety layers are built.

Problem:

Manual intervention may take time and we may miss opportunities. Therefore we need a professional fast-execution workflow.

Goal:

Build a professional Swing Cash Live-Readiness and Fast Execution system that can:

1. Detect valid swing opportunities automatically.
2. Validate data, risk, liquidity, sector exposure, event risk, and entry validity.
3. Auto-stage orders instantly.
4. Provide one-click approval for the owner.
5. Support optional guarded auto-execution later, only after paper/dry-run proof.
6. Never place live orders blindly without safety gates.
7. Never use fake, stale, delayed, or untrusted data for trade-grade decisions.

====================================================
ABSOLUTE RULES
==============

Do not activate live trading by default.

Do not place real broker orders during this task unless explicitly approved after dry-run verification.

Do not change swing scoring logic blindly.

Do not change entry/SL/target logic blindly.

Do not loosen risk controls.

Do not use Yahoo/delayed/untrusted data for trade-grade swing entries.

Do not silently replace Kite/trusted data with fallback data.

Do not delete useful production features.

Do not run destructive DB migrations.

Do not expose broker tokens, Kite tokens, auth cookies, passwords, or secrets in logs.

Do not touch F&O engine, F&O risk guards, F&O scoring, option-chain math, capital ledger, or F&O paper trading unless directly required for shared broker-safety infrastructure.

This task is for Swing Cash Segment only.

Everything must be professional-grade, tested, auditable, and production-safe.

* total swing capital

* risk per trade %
* entry price
* stop price
* risk per share

Output:

* recommended quantity
* capital required
* max loss if SL hit
* max loss with gap buffer
* risk %
* position value %

Rules:

1. Quantity must be risk-based, not only slot-based.
2. Respect max capital per trade.
3. Respect max loss per trade.
4. Respect available cash.
5. Respect reserve cash.
6. If stop distance is too wide, reduce quantity or block.
7. If quantity too small, mark as not worth trading.

Example:

If capital = ₹10,00,000 and risk = 0.5%, max risk = ₹5,000.

If entry = ₹1,000 and SL = ₹950, risk/share = ₹50.

Max qty by risk = 100 shares.

Then cap by max position value too.

Add tests for:

* normal sizing
* wide stop reducing qty

* reserve cash
* max position cap
