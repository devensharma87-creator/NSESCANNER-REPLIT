The audit confirms the site uses multiple layers — live Kite, delayed Yahoo, NSE archive/context data, cached scanner data, and computed/derived data — and therefore needs a single canonical truth layer so users do not see different information in different tabs.

The deterministic parity harness is designed to solve the repeated problem where final parity was dependent on live market hours.
