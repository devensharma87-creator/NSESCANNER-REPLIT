The Swing staged-order Telegram alert fixes are complete in dev and now need production verification after publish.

Two important fixes were made:

Pricing label/source honesty fix

Old misleading message:

Data: kite

Problem:

The staged entry price was owner-supplied limit price.
It was not the current Kite LTP.
Example: RELIANCE entry was ₹1,400, while Kite LTP was around ₹1,293.90.
The old label could mislead the owner into thinking the entry price was Kite market price.

New correct message:

Risk eval: kite (as of <time>)
Note: Entry is the staged limit order price — not current market price

Manual expire alert fix
manuallyExpireSwingOrder now fires SWING_ORDER_EXPIRED.
Earlier only TTL expiry path fired expiry alert.
