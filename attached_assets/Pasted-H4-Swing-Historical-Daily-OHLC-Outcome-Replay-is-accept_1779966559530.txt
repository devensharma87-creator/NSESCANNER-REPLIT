## Questions to answer

1. Why are high-score rows underperforming lower-score rows?

Check whether the high-score group is biased toward:

* overextended stocks,
* high RSI,
* late-stage momentum,
* poor reward after move already happened,
* entry too close to exhaustion,
* weak forward breadth,
* high volatility,
* low sector confirmation,
* missing RS data,
* weak market context,
* formulaic RR,
* post-breakout failure risk.

2. Why are WATCH/BUY rows underperforming AVOID?

Check whether AVOID rows include many technically strong stocks that are rejected only because of:

* action gate,
* RR gate,
* bearish penalty,
* entry safety demotion,
* overextension warning,
* score/action mismatch,
* quality-grade mismatch,
* trigger not actionable,
* label logic too conservative or inconsistent.

3. Is the action label reliable?

Audit:

* `classifyAction`
* score thresholds,
* RR gates,
* buy-zone / breakout conditions,
* warning penalties,
* recommendation labels,
* “AVOID / NO TRADE” conditions,
* “WAIT FOR CONFIRMATION”
* “WATCHLIST”
* “WAIT FOR PULLBACK”
* “BUY BREAKOUT”
* “BUY ZONE - WAIT TRIGGER”

4. Is the final score reliable?

Break down performance by:

* technical score,
* SMC score,
* VWAP/volume/RS bucket,
* momentum score,
* fundamental score,
* context score,
* risk/RR score,
* RSI bucket,
* volume ratio bucket,
* RS bucket,
* sector leader class,
* entry distance bucket,
* stop distance bucket,
* RR bucket.

5. Is the scanner selecting candidates too late?

For H4 rows, check:

* distance from 20-day high,
* distance from 52-week high,
* RSI level,
* ATR/volatility,
* whether forward return mean-reverts after scan,
* whether high-score rows are already extended.

6. Is RR too mechanical?

H4 showed many rows have RR around 2.00.

Audit whether:

* targets are mostly formulaic,
* RR is not truly predictive,
* stop/target placement creates false confidence,
* target1 is too far or too close,
* stop is too wide or too tight.

7. Is sector leadership useful despite current score issues?

Use H3/H4 to check:

* leader vs inline vs laggard outcomes,
* whether leader overlay would reduce false positives,
* whether AVOID ∩ leader rows performed better,
* whether WATCHLIST ∩ leader rows performed better,
* whether laggard rows should receive penalties.
