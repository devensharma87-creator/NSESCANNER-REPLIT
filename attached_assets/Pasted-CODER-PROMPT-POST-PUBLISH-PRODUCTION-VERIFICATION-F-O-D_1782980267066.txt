CODER PROMPT — POST-PUBLISH PRODUCTION VERIFICATION: F&O DAILY HISTORY FRESHNESS FIX

The F&O daily-history path fix has been completed in dev.

Reported root cause:

Kite was returning daily candles correctly, but the candle freshness policy was wrong.

The candle functions were using:

lastTsSec * 1000

meaning the timestamp of the last daily candle bar, often yesterday’s candle open time, as asOfMs.

Because the market-data stale budget is around 600 seconds, yesterday’s completed daily candle looked stale by 20+ hours.

Result:

Kite returned 60 daily bars.
buildMeta() marked them hard-stale.
isTradeableMeta became false.
router returned null.
warmup threw daily_history_unavailable_kite.
backbone showed dailyBars UNKNOWN.
F&O remained blocked even with active Kite session.

Fix implemented:

getIndexCandles
getEquityCandles
getEquityCandlesByToken

now use fetch time:

Date.now()

as the candle series asOfMs.

This means freshness means “how recently the candle series was fetched from Kite,” not “what date the latest completed daily candle represents.”
