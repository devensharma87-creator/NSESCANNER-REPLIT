Here are some of the more reliable intraday strategies for index F&O on Nifty, Bank Nifty, and Sensex. A quick caveat first: no strategy "works in all timeframes on all indices" without adaptation — Bank Nifty/Sensex are more volatile than Nifty, so position sizing and stop distances must change even when the logic stays the same.

1. Opening Range Breakout (ORB) Mark the high and low of the first 15 or 30 minutes. Go long on a candle close above the range high, short on a close below the low. Works across all three because index opens often set the day's directional tone. Use the opposite end of the range as your stop. Best in the 5-min and 15-min frames. Avoid trading the breakout if VIX is very low (range-bound days) — false breakouts are common.

2. VWAP Trend / Reversion VWAP is the single most useful intraday level for indices because institutional flow respects it.

Trend day: price holds above VWAP → buy pullbacks to VWAP. Below VWAP → sell rallies into it.
Range day: fade moves that stretch far from VWAP (1+ standard deviation bands) back toward it. The 5-min works for entries, 15-min for bias. Same logic on all three indices.
3. EMA Pullback (9/21 EMA) On the 5-min or 15-min, when the 9 EMA is above the 21 EMA (uptrend), buy the first pullback that touches the 21 EMA and shows a rejection candle. Reverse for downtrends. Simple, robust, and timeframe-agnostic. Combine with VWAP for confirmation.

4. Support/Resistance + Price Action at Round Numbers Indices respect prior-day high/low, the day's PDH/PDL, and psychological round levels (Nifty 25000, Bank Nifty 52000, etc.). Wait for a clear rejection wick or engulfing candle at these zones rather than entering blind. Higher win-rate but fewer trades.

5. Momentum with RSI + Volume On breakouts, confirm with RSI above 60 (longs) / below 40 (shorts) and a volume spike. Filters out the weak breakouts that trap traders. Note: Sensex volume data on some platforms is unreliable, so lean on price/RSI there rather than volume.

A few things that matter more than the strategy itself:

Match volatility to size. Bank Nifty can move 300+ points in minutes; size down versus Nifty.
Trade with the trend on trend days, fade on range days. Use the first 30–45 min and VIX to decide which regime you're in.
Avoid the lunch lull (roughly 12:00–1:30) — low volume, choppy, most false signals happen here.
Hard stop-loss always, since F&O leverage cuts both ways fast.
