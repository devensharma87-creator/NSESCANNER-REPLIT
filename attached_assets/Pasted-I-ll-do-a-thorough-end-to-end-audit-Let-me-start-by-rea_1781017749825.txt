The options layer is NOT real — it is a fixed linear proxy. This is the most important finding:

Entry premium = exactly 0.400% of spot for every single trade (zero variance across all 1,450 rows). Real ATM premiums swing with days-to-expiry and IV; a flat 0.40% is a placeholder.

So entry_spot/exit_spot are real; entry_prem/exit_prem are manufactured from them. This matches the "delta proxy behavior / theta modeling" gap already flagged in your top-of-mind notes, and it's why Real Replay (captured chain) and Strategy Research (this synthetic data) aren't comparable.
Reliability of P&L: because premiums are synthetic, the P&L is directional-edge-on-a-proxy, not a tradeable options backtest. It will systematically overstate option-buying performance (no theta bleed, no IV crush, no slippage on illiquid strikes).
