P0-3 — SIGNAL CARD WORDING VS LIFECYCLE TRIGGER SEMANTICS

====================================================
WHY THIS PHASE IS REQUIRED
====================================================

The audit found that F&O signal cards say things like:

“15-min close above entry level”

But the lifecycle engine appears to trigger paper entries on touch/wick/high-low:

CALL: bar high >= entry
PUT: bar low <= entry

This creates a serious honesty mismatch:

1. Human sees close-confirmed strategy.
2. Paper engine executes touch-trigger strategy.
3. Paper trades may enter on wick pokes that would not satisfy the card wording.
4. Reported paper results may not match the displayed trade plan.
5. Future live-trading transition would be unsafe if wording and execution differ.

This must be fixed so there is exactly one truth.

====================================================
PART B — DECISION REQUIRED
====================================================

Choose the safer implementation for this phase.

Preferred for low-risk fix:

OPTION B — Keep existing touch/wick execution, but make wording honest.

Reason:
- Changing execution to close-confirmed will materially alter live/paper behavior.
- Touch execution is already what historical paper records used.
- A wording fix is lower risk and makes the system truthful immediately.
- A later separate phase can add close-confirmed mode behind a feature flag.

Implement:

1. Rename card wording from:
   “15-min close above/below entry”
   to:
   “Spot touches / crosses entry level”
   or:
   “Trigger: touch of entry level within monitored candle/tick”

2. Add explicit field:

triggerSemantics = "TOUCH_OR_TICK"

3. Add explanation:

“Paper engine triggers when the monitored candle high/low or live tick touches the entry level. This is not close-confirmed.”

4. If any strategy truly requires close confirmation, mark:

triggerSemantics = "CLOSE_CONFIRMED"

but do not claim close-confirmed unless the engine actually waits for candle close.
