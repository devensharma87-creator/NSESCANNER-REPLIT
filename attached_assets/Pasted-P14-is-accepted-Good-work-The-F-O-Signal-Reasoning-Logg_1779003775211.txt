However, one limitation is important:

The logger currently captures the **paper-trade decision boundary**, but it does **not yet capture the upstream signal-generation reasoning inside `optionSignals.ts` / confluence generation**. So we can see why a signal was opened, skipped, stopped, or closed, but we still cannot fully see why the signal originally appeared from the signal engine.
