**Index Futures Net OI**
`Future Index Long - Future Index Short`

**Stock Futures Net OI**
`Future Stock Long - Future Stock Short`

**Index Options Directional Net OI**
`(Index Call Long + Index Put Short) - (Index Call Short + Index Put Long)`

**Stock Options Directional Net OI**
`(Stock Call Long + Stock Put Short) - (Stock Call Short + Stock Put Long)`

Do not calculate options net as simply:

`Total Option Long - Total Option Short`

That can give the wrong directional reading because calls and puts have opposite directional meaning.
