Required functions:

- isTradingDate(exchange, date)
- getTradingSession(exchange, date)
- getLatestCompletedTradingSession(exchange, now)
- getPreviousTradingSession(exchange, date)
- validateBhavcopySession(exchange, bhavcopyDate, now)

Rules:

1. Use Asia/Kolkata consistently.
2. A session is completed only after its official scheduled close.
3. During pre-open or market hours, latest completed means the previous completed session.
4. After official close, today may become the latest completed session only when the required official session output is available and valid.
5. Weekends are closed unless an official special session overrides them.
6. Official holidays are closed.
7. Special/Muhurat sessions override ordinary weekend/holiday assumptions.
8. Unknown year, missing source, conflicting circulars or invalid dates fail closed.
9. Future-dated bhavcopy/UDiFF fails closed.
10. A UDiFF date must equal the calculated latest completed BSE session required by policy.
11. Do not introduce an arbitrary hourly freshness threshold.
12. Calendar authority does not make quotes LIVE.

D. SOURCE CONFLICT AND UPDATE POLICY

- Preserve both sources and the override reason.
- Reject equal-priority contradictory sources.
- Never silently choose one.
