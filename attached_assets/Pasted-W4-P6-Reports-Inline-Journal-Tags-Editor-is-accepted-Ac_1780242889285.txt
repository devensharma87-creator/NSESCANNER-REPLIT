* symbol/index,
* MFE,

Null/NaN last.

No mutation.


Suggested columns:

* date,
* segment,
* symbol/index,
* setup,
* exit reason,
* realised P&L,
* R multiple,
* journal,
* tags.

Rules:

* use `serializeReportRowsToCsv`,

* escape commas/quotes/newlines,
* generate browser download only,
* no backend call,
