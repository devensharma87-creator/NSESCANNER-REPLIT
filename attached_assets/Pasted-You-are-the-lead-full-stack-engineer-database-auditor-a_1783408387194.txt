Definitions to use in the report:

A. Signal Triggered:
A signal candidate was generated by any F&O signal detector, strategy, setup, baseline engine, or bias engine before final approval.

B. Signal Passed:
A triggered signal passed the mandatory filters/gates and became eligible for paper trading or alerting.

C. Signal Rejected / Skipped:
A triggered signal failed due to gate, confidence, HTF veto, data issue, option-chain issue, risk rule, duplicate rule, cooldown, session rule, expiry rule, spread/liquidity rule, or any other reason.

D. Trade Taken:
A passed signal resulted in an actual paper-trade record or paper position being opened.

E. Trade Not Taken:
A signal passed but did not become a paper trade. The reason must be identified.

F. P&L:
Profit/loss must be calculated using option premium entry and option premium exit wherever available. Do not calculate F&O trade P&L using only index spot movement. If the system used spot as proxy, flag it as unreliable.

G. Open Trade:
A paper trade/position that has not been exited yet.

H. Closed Trade:
A paper trade/position with valid exit time and exit price/premium.

Required calculations:
For every closed trade:

Gross P&L for long option:
`(exit_option_premium - entry_option_premium) * quantity`

If quantity is stored in lots:
`(exit_option_premium - entry_option_premium) * lot_size * lots`

Net P&L:
`gross_pnl - brokerage - taxes - slippage - other_costs`

If costs are unavailable:
Show gross P&L and mark net P&L as “cost data unavailable”.

R multiple:
For long option:
`(exit_option_premium - entry_option_premium) / (entry_option_premium - stop_loss_premium)`

If stop-loss premium missing:
Mark R multiple as “cannot calculate”.

MFE:
`highest_option_premium_after_entry - entry_option_premium`

MAE:
`lowest_option_premium_after_entry - entry_option_premium`

Win rate:
`winning closed trades / total closed trades`

Profit factor:
`gross profit / absolute gross loss`

Expectancy:
`(win_rate * average_win) - (loss_rate * average_loss)`

Drawdown:
Calculate from cumulative closed-trade P&L in chronological order.

Important P&L rule:
If the database only has spot entry/exit but not option premium entry/exit, do not call the P&L reliable.
