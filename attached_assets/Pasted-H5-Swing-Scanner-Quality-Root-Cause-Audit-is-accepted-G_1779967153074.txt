## Models to simulate

Compare the current live score/action baseline against multiple offline shadow models.

At minimum include:

### M0 — Current baseline

Current score/action exactly as stored.

### M1 — Remove or heavily downweight `fundamental_score`

Reason: H5 found `fundamental_score` had the strongest negative relationship with forward return.

Test:

* remove it,
* cap it,
* or reduce its weight materially.

Do not implement this in code. Simulation only.

### M2 — Overextension-aware model

Add stronger penalties for:

* high RSI,
* overextended from moving averages,
* near exhaustion,
* warning labels such as `warn_extended` / `warn_rsi_overext`,
* poor forward MAE risk.

### M3 — Sector-leader overlay

Use H3/H4 leader class:

* leader bonus,
* laggard penalty,
* inline neutral.

This is simulation only, not S4c implementation.

### M4 — RR de-emphasis

Remove RR as a predictive score component, or reduce it sharply.

Reason: H5 found RR is formulaic and clustered around 2.0–2.5R.

RR may remain useful for sizing/risk, but not as a strong score driver.

### M5 — Weak-warning gate repair simulation

Test alternative handling of weak-warning AVOID gates:

* do not automatically demote all weak-warning rows to AVOID,
* distinguish real weakness from mean-reversion candidates,
* separately test `warn_rs_weak` as a stronger negative gate.

### M6 — Combined candidate model

Combine the best conservative pieces from M1–M5.

Do not overfit.

Keep it simple and explainable.

## Required analysis

For each model:

1. Calculate shadow score or shadow rank.

2. Compare against current score.

3. Group rows into score buckets.

4. Measure forward returns:

   * 1D
   * 3D
   * 5D
   * 10D if enough data
   * MFE
   * MAE

5. Check monotonicity:

   * Does higher shadow score produce better forward return?
   * Pearson/Spearman correlation if feasible.
   * Bucket ladder by score quintile.

6. Compare action support:

   * current WATCH/BUY rows,
   * current AVOID rows,
   * sector leaders,
   * high-score losers,
   * low-score winners.

7. Check whether the model reduces H4/H5 red flags:

   * WATCH/BUY underperforming AVOID,
   * high score underperforming low score,
   * AVOID containing hidden winners,
   * overextended high-score failures,
   * formulaic RR false confidence.
